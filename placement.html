Part E – Speaking (teacher assessed, 0–5)

Interview (4–6 minutes): 1. Introduce yourself and describe a typical day. 2. Talk about a useful place in your community. 3. Explain an improvement you would make and why. 4. Follow-up: How might different age groups disagree about this?

The teacher should ask follow-up questions spontaneously; do not judge from prepared notes alone.

Teacher scoring (after interview)

Use the 0–5 rubric: 0 = not assessable; 1 = very limited; 2 = basic; 3 = functional; 4 = effective; 5 = consistently clear, flexible and accurate. Consider task response, organisation, vocabulary and grammar for Writing; fluency, interaction, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation for Speaking.
